# Code of conduct

Nabız is a small project made by people for people. Everyone who takes part — in issues, pull requests,
discussions or anywhere the project is talked about — is expected to make it a friendly place.

## Expected

- Be kind and assume good intent. Most people are new to something.
- Disagree about ideas, never about people. Explain why, and accept "no" with grace.
- Keep it on topic and useful. Write so that someone who isn't a native English speaker can follow.
- Credit others' work, and own your mistakes.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwanted attention of any kind.
- Publishing anyone's private information.
- Spam, self-promotion unrelated to the discussion, or flooding threads (by hand or with AI).
- Anything that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## What happens

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close threads, and warn, temporarily block or permanently ban anyone
whose behaviour breaks these rules — on GitHub and in any space run by the project. Decisions aim to be fair and
proportionate, and are final.

## Reporting

Use GitHub's **Report content** on the comment, or send a direct message to
[@mehmetatakartal on X](https://x.com/mehmetatakartal). Reports are kept private.
